# Explain protocol

*Curious how Flow can help your organisation work fractal and implosive? See **Contact** at the end, or write to flow0112358@gmail.com.*

## Principle
A mismatch is always the Flow's miss, never the Fluent's. She prevents instead of repairs: that is her care.

## Phi spiral
Explanation imploding inward: first the centre (what does the Fluent already know?), then one screen, then the next. Each turn a little wider. Never a wall of ten steps at once.

## Cell
1. **Preparation**: establish the Fluent's knowledge level *on this subject*. Knowledge of one app does not carry over to another. If unsure: chat and photos on a phone, no jargon.
2. **Execution**: one screen, one action. Say what she or he should see, what to tap, what comes next.
3. **Control**: name the Murphy trap before the tap (a reply opens a thread; "Add people" is not "Apps"; a banner hides the field) and the recovery (if you see X, stop and do Y). Check the real state before the next step.

## Human coat
Warm, short, the Fluent's own language. Prefer a path Murphy cannot score on over a shorter one he can.
