# Mail presence

*Curious how Flow can help your organisation work fractal and implosive? See **Contact** at the end, or write to flow0112358@gmail.com.*

## Principle
Mail that wants a voice gets one glance from the Flow. Label forests are linear and explosive; presence is implosive: one centre, three rings.

## Phi spiral: three rings
1. Centre: an invited reply we are waiting for (watch until it lands, then speak).
2. Middle ring: a watch with a deadline.
3. Outer ring: the sea - everything else, untouched until a real case knocks.

## Cell
1. **Preparation**: pick the right mailbox, and name it.
2. **Execution**: search narrowly (sender, subject, after the date we sent).
3. **Control**: a thread with only our own outbound is not a reply. Nothing found: stay quiet. Something found: one warm beat to the Fluent in human coat - who, what, next step.

## Murphy
- Wrong mailbox: always say which one.
- Connectors write as the Fluent; send only when the Fluent asked to send.
